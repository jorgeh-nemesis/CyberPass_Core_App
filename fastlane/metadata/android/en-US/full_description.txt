CyberPass is a fully offline, open-source password manager designed for maximum privacy and security.

Features:
- Offline-first: No internet permission, no cloud sync, no tracking.
- Strong Encryption: AES-GCM encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation.
- Biometric Unlock: Local authentication using Android Keystore.
- Autofill Support: Fill credentials seamlessly in apps and browsers.
